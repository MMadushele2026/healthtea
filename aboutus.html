Our History

FST (Flat Stomach Tea) began as a small,home-grown retail business selling custom tea and capsule combos directly to our local community.what started through word-of-mouth recommendations among friends and family quickly expanded into an online store dedicated to making quality wellness accessible to everyone.

Mission Statement

To provide affordable, high-quality,and easy-to-use wellness tea and capsule combos that help everyday South Africans support their digestion, reduce bloating,and feel confident every day

Vision Statement

To become a trusted, recognisable wellness retail brand celebrated for genuine product,transparent ingredient information, amnd outstanding customer care.
